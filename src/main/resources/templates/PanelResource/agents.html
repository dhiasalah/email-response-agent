Specialized agents

The router gives every email to one agent. Each agent writes with its own instructions and reads only its own context: its knowledge base, its calendar, and the answers learned from its own emails.

How an email finds its agent

Evidence adds up: listed sender 1.0, sender domain 0.5, sender role 0.4, each keyword 0.3 (at most 0.6), same thread as an earlier email 0.8. Below 0.3 no agent fits and the fallback agent answers. When two agents are within 0.1, the one listed first wins and the decision is marked as a close call. A members-only agent never takes an email from someone outside its members, whatever the email says.
